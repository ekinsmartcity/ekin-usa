## Privacy Policy (`#privacy`)

### 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Ekin Inc. (“Ekin,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and discloses personal information when you visit this website, contact us, request a demonstration or quote, ask for support, submit a partnership inquiry, subscribe to updates, or otherwise interact with us through this website. Separate notices or agreements may apply to customer deployments, products, employment applications, and other services. In a customer deployment, the customer may control the information collected through its Ekin products; this Policy does not replace that customer's privacy notice or contract.

### 2. Information we collect

**Information you provide.** Depending on your interaction, we may collect your name, business email address, telephone number, company, job title, country, inquiry category, message, communication preferences, and correspondence with us. Please do not include sensitive personal information in an inquiry unless it is necessary and requested.

**Information collected through the website.** Our website and service providers may collect IP address, device and browser information, referring page, pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP address, and interaction and diagnostic information through server logs, cookies, and similar technologies. The specific technologies in use and your available choices should be described in the site's cookie controls.

**Information from other sources.** We may receive business contact information from organizations you represent, event organizers, partners, or publicly available professional sources, where appropriate for our business relationship.

### 3. How we use information

We use information to respond to requests; arrange demonstrations and provide product information; manage business relationships and support requests; operate, protect, and improve the website; measure site performance; send updates or marketing communications where permitted and in accordance with your preferences; and comply with applicable law and protect legal rights. You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in those messages.

### 4. Disclosures

We may disclose information to providers that support website hosting, analytics, communications, customer relationship management, security, and other business operations; to affiliated companies or business partners where needed to respond to your request; and to authorities or other parties when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights and safety. We may also disclose information in connection with a corporate transaction, subject to applicable law. Our providers are expected to use information for the services they perform for us and under appropriate contractual protections.

Do not state that Ekin “never sells” or “never shares” information for targeted advertising until the actual advertising and analytics configuration has been checked under applicable state-law definitions.

### 5. Cookies and choices

You can manage cookies through any preference controls offered on the website and your browser settings. Some technologies are needed for the website to function. Analytics and advertising technologies, if used, should be described accurately in the cookie interface and subject to choices required by applicable law. Blocking some cookies may affect site features. If the website uses targeted advertising or practices that constitute a “sale” or “sharing” under applicable state law, provide the required opt-out mechanism and honor applicable opt-out preference signals.

### 6. Retention and security

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including to manage our business relationship, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the website. Retention depends on the type of information and the relevant purpose. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

### 7. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may be entitled to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information; obtain information about its use or disclosure; opt out of certain processing; or appeal a decision about a privacy request. We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law, subject to identity verification and legal exceptions. To submit a request, contact **legal@ekin.com**. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify and process it. If another organization controls information generated by an Ekin product deployment, please direct requests about that deployment to that organization.

This general paragraph does not replace any state-specific notice or opt-out interface required after the website's actual practices and legal applicability are assessed.

Links to third-party sites are governed by those sites' own policies. We may revise this Policy and will post the updated version with its effective date.
